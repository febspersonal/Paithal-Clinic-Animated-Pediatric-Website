WELCOME TO PAITHAL

A small space for big beginnings.

Paithal Clinic is a paediatric specialty clinic in Cherthala, Alappuzha, led by Dr. Neethu Mohan. We provide specialized healthcare for infants and children, with individual attention, professionalism and empathy at every visit.
